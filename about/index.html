About Flight Hotel Package

Updated November 10, 2026

Booking sites advertise package savings, but the saving depends on the trip. Flight Hotel Package exists to help you check: when bundling the flight and hotel tends to be cheaper, when it isn't, what packages leave out of the headline price, and how timing changes the answer.

Who's behind it

The site is published by Eye To Ad Media, an independent web publisher in Denver, Colorado, run by Zach Wennstedt. We're not a travel agent or a booking engine. When you choose to book, you do it on Expedia.

Our approach

  • Compare totals, not headlines. We always point to transfers, bags, resort fees and cancellation terms, because that's where packages differ.
  • Say when not to bundle. Multi-stop trips, very short stays and some peak dates often work out better booked separately.
  • No invented numbers. We don't publish fake "you'll save X%" claims; the calculator uses your own prices.

Funding

We earn Expedia affiliate commissions when readers book through our links, and we may show ads. See the disclosure. Spot an error? Let us know.
