Privacy and Affiliate Disclosure

Updated November 10, 2026

Flight Hotel Package (flighthotelpackage.com) compares booking a flight and hotel as a bundle against booking them separately, with a calculator and guides on timing. No account is needed. Here's what happens to data when you use it.

The bundle calculator

The calculator works in your browser. The prices you type are not sent to us or stored by us.

Expedia affiliate search and links

Search widgets and "Book on Expedia" links on this site come from the Expedia Group affiliate program. If you use them, Expedia Group and its affiliate-tracking partners may set or read cookies to record that you arrived from this site, and if you book we may receive a commission at no extra cost to you. Searches and bookings happen on Expedia under its privacy statement, and we never see your booking or payment details.

Commissions don't change our conclusions: plenty of our pages tell you when a package isn't worth it.

Analytics

Google Analytics 4 sets cookies to measure visits: pages, device, approximate region and referring site. We look at the totals to improve the guides. GitHub Pages, our host, may log basic request data for security.

Advertising

If Google ads appear, Google and third-party vendors use cookies to serve ads based on your visits here and elsewhere. Opt out of personalized ads at Google Ads Settings or aboutads.info; read how Google uses partner-site data.

Choices, children, changes

You can refuse or clear cookies in your browser. The site isn't intended for children under 13. If this policy changes, so does the date above. Questions go to the contact page.
